Architecture
as atmosphere.

Client NEVA Zipscreen
Work Architecture · Visualization
Type CGI · Architectural Vis

Architectural visualization as atmosphere — a residential project rendered at the intersection of raw concrete, soft light, and landscape silence.

The brief called for images that felt less like renders and more like photographs taken on a quiet morning — before the building had been lived in, before it had a history. Every frame was built around that idea: stillness, weight, and the kind of light that doesn't try to impress.
