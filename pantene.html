Client Pantene
Work Pro-V Miracle · Beauty CGI
Type CGI · Campaign

A full CGI campaign for Pantene Pro-V Miracle — hero shots built on motion, fluid dynamics, and surfaces that feel like they're still moving.

The visual language of this campaign was built around transformation: the moment just before stillness, when a liquid is interrupted mid-flow. Each frame was designed to hold tension — beautiful but not quite at rest. Houdini simulations drove the fluid behavior; Redshift handled the rest.
